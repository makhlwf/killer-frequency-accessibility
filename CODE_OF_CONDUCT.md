# Community conduct

Treat players and contributors with respect. Discuss accessibility barriers without blaming the person who encountered them. Use clear language, mark spoilers, and welcome reports from people using different screen readers or input devices.

Harassment, threats, discriminatory abuse, doxxing and deliberate publication of private information are not acceptable. Keep contributions relevant and constructive. Maintainers may edit or remove harmful content and restrict participation when needed.

Report ordinary project issues through GitHub. For sensitive security reports, follow SECURITY.md. Do not include private information in public reports. This is a volunteer project; timely responses are not guaranteed.
